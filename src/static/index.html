Municipal Waste Sorting Standard Matrix

Standardized color codes, accepted polymer / material types, and contamination hazards.

🧴

Plastic (Blue/Yellow Bin)

Codes #1 PETE, #2 HDPE, #4 LDPE, #5 PP

Bottles, detergent tubs, beverage containers. Rinse and re-attach screw caps.

Never include: Soft thin shopping bags or black NIR-invisible food trays.

📦

Paper & Cardboard (Blue Bin)

Cardboard, Newspapers, Office Sheets

Keep dry and flatten boxes. Remove packing tapes and Styrofoam.

Never include: Greasy pizza boxes or BPA thermal cash register receipts.

🥫

Metals & Cans (Yellow Bin)

ALU 41 Beverage Cans, FE 40 Tin Food Cans

100% infinitely recyclable. Empty residue and ball up clean aluminum foil.

Never include: Pressurized gas aerosols unless fully emptied.

🍾

Glass Bottles (Green/Brown Bank)

GL 70, GL 71, GL 72 Jars & Bottles

Rinse and remove metal caps. Infinitely recyclable with no purity loss.

Never include: Pyrex oven glass, ceramics, or lightbulbs.

🍏

Organic Compost (Green/Brown Bin)

Food Peels, Coffee Grounds, Yard Trimmings

Eliminates landfill methane. Compost into nutrient-rich agricultural humus.

Never include: Fruit PLU barcode plastic stickers or non-compostable wraps.

💻

E-Waste & Batteries (Hazardous Drop-Off)

Li-Ion, Circuit Boards, Mobile Phones

Contains gold, copper, and silver. Tape battery terminals before drop-off.

Never include: In normal household bins (puncture fire hazard!).
